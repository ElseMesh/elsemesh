"Godzilla 2014" (https://skfb.ly/pC9tn) by Steam79 is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The game export was adapted in Blender from the downloaded GLB: geometry was reduced, the model was scaled to 24 metres, texture resolution was reduced, and a simple walk rig and animation were added. The original download is not included in this repository. See `tools/blender/prepare_godzilla.py` for the conversion.

Godzilla is an existing fictional character. The model creator's CC BY licence may not cover rights in the underlying character; this local game prototype has not been cleared for public release.
