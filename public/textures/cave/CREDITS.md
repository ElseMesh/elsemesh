# UNDERNEATH cave stone

Integration: **Agent Control**, 27 September 2026.

[Rock Boulder Dry](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), Poly Haven.
Photography: Dimitrios Savva. Processing: Rico Cilliers.
Licence: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

Unmodified 1K JPEG diffuse, OpenGL normal, and packed AO/roughness/metal maps.
Downloaded from the official Poly Haven texture API/CDN. Runtime albedo is sRGB;
normal and ARM maps are linear, with full mip chains. World projection repeats
at the source's 1.8 metre scale. Wet darkening is performed by the shader.
No 4K/8K textures are required for the mobile profile.
