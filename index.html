Tip

Take the boat around the western headland. The sheltered cave entrance leads to a hidden station.

Open the cave's final door with E, then board the undersea monorail to a second island.

Press N for the three-island map. Sail south past Godzilla to Loz's Helicopter Rental, collect a key with E, and follow the path to the helipad. In the helicopter: WASD to fly, Space/C for altitude, mouse to steer and left/right cursor keys to look through the side windows.

Read the expedition log in Station B, then find the silent signal mast above ground.

Press G to equip the sidearm, click to fire and press X to reload.

Fish to survive: keep a catch in the cooler and press B to prepare and eat it.

The sky follows your local time. Open Sky settings to preview sunset, moon and stars at any time.

The monorail runs both ways. You can return to the home island and boat.
