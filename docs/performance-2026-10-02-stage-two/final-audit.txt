Conservative interpretation
- The combined revised switches improve pooled observed FPS by 2.6666%, supported by positive pooled results in both cycles. Largest repeatable gains are beach and underwater.
- Villa is 0.63% slower and warehouse 0.49% slower, in both cycles; cargo is effectively unchanged. These small losses are retained, not declared wins or removed. No statistical significance claim is made from two cycles.
- Pooled >150 ms hitches are zero in both modes and worst frame falls, but tail improvement is not universal: underwater p99 increases 50.5 -> 66.5 ms and villa 50.3 -> 66.4 ms.
- This does not establish that each of the three switches independently improves FPS. The earlier water-only villa result was neutral on average with worse tails; old-code outcomes cannot qualify the revised shader.
- Prior failed combined/depth-sort and pre-fix safeCombined runs remain diagnostic history. Their frames are not pooled into this changed-source result, and their hitches must not be silently discarded from that history.
- The flags-off baseline is the revised source with common refactors already present, including water shader scaffolding and forest batch construction. This comparison establishes runtime-switch benefit, not a complete source-to-source comparison against the published 875a67e build.
- Fixed-camera dt=0 screening does not establish live-gameplay, moving-camera transitions, long-session stability, thermals, other hardware, High/SSR, or all-profile FPS. Separate visual review and any exact published-source comparison remain separate evidence.
- Promotion of only the three default booleans is consistent with the measured combination; maintain opaque depth sorting off. Record the final shipped hashes separately after any promotion. A claim of universal FPS or hitch improvement would exceed this evidence.

Integrity failures: none after exact verification of the declared post-measurement changes.
